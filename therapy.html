About the initiative

Real therapy, made affordable

Kirana Counselling trains Master’s students and early-career therapists through a structured 6-month programme with supervised, hands-on practice. After three months of rigorous training, our trainees offer free and low-cost therapy sessions. Every trainee therapist works under active supervision, so you get good-quality care.

  • Adults only (18+ years of age)
  • All sessions are online
  • Languages: Hindi & English
  • First 3 sessions free, then ₹299 per session. Affordable sessions are available for a limited period. Your trainee therapist will explain this on the intro call.
  1. Fill the form2 minutes

  3. Intro callA vibe check with your therapist

We are not a crisis helpline, and our services aren’t suited for clinical mental health conditions or sexual wellness concerns. If any of these come up, we’ll refer you to the right kind of support.
